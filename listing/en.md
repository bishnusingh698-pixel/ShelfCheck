# App Store listing — English (en)

App name: **ShelfCheck: Duplicate SKU & Catalog Audit**
Tagline: Find and fix catalog problems before they stop a shipment.
Introduction: ShelfCheck scans your catalog for duplicate SKUs and barcodes,
missing fields, and pricing mistakes, then shows you exactly what to fix.
Every check runs on a schedule or on demand, and every result is explained in
plain language with a direct link to the product in your Shopify admin.
Feature bullets:
- Detects duplicate SKUs, duplicate barcodes, and invalid GS1 check digits
- Flags missing images, weights, and costs, and zero-price mistakes
- Explains each issue in plain language with a link straight to the product
- In-app activity feed shows what changed since your last visit
- Exports any filtered list to CSV for your team to work through
Short description: A catalog audit that finds duplicate SKUs, bad barcodes,
and missing data — and explains how to fix them.

## Plans (Shopify App Pricing — exact dashboard setup)

- **Free** — $0. Scheduled monthly scan, up to 500 variants, newest 50
  issues, 7 days of activity history, CSV export (50 rows).
- **Starter** — $4.99/month, 7-day free trial. Scheduled weekly scan, 3
  manual scans/day, up to 5,000 variants, all issues, webhook watchers,
  weekly summary, 30 days of activity history, CSV export.
- **Pro** — $9.99/month, 7-day free trial. Scheduled daily scan, 10 manual
  scans/day, up to 50,000 variants, all issues, webhook watchers, weekly
  summary, auto-tagging, 90 days of activity history, CSV export.

(No outbound messages of any kind — no email, SMS, push, or chat alerts.
All updates appear in the in-app activity feed.)
